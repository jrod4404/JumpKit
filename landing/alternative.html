FAQ

Alternative questions answered.

What makes JumpKit a good alternative to a bookmark manager?

JumpKit does what a bookmark manager does — organizes your links into searchable categories — but it goes further by also launching local folders and network shares, supporting team sharing, tracking time saved, and keeping all data 100% local on your machine.

Is my data stored in the cloud with JumpKit?

No. JumpKit stores everything locally on your machine by default. Optional auto-backup is available when you want it, but your data never has to leave your device or network.

Can cloud bookmark managers open local folders or network shares?

No. Cloud bookmark managers store web links in the cloud and only open web URLs. JumpKit is a desktop app that opens web links, local folders, and network shares in one click.

How do I migrate my bookmarks to JumpKit?

Export your current bookmark URLs, then add them as JumpKit jumps in the relevant category. You can also start from your most-used links. Because JumpKit stores data locally, there is no lock-in either way.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. JumpKit Free includes the core launcher with web links, local folders, categories, search, filters, hotkeys, and a personal ROI dashboard. JumpKit Unlimited ($10/month or $99/year) adds unlimited jumps, team sharing, auto-archive, and auto-backup.
