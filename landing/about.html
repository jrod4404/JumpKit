About

Stop searching. Start jumping.

JumpKit is a local-first desktop launcher for Windows and macOS that gives you one-click access to the web links, folders, and network shares you use every day — built for knowledge workers, IT & MSP teams, and small businesses.

JumpKit was born out of a simple frustration: we spend too much time looking for things we've already found.

Every day, knowledge workers navigate the same web links, the same shared folders, the same internal resources — over and over. Some live in browser bookmarks. Some in chat messages. Some in email signatures. Some in Notion docs. The result is wasted time, fragmented workflows, and the constant question: "Where did I put that link?"

Our mission: give people their time back. Every unnecessary click, every folder dive, every "let me find that link" message — that's time stolen from real work. JumpKit tracks the savings so you can see exactly how much it matters.

What JumpKit Does

Jumps — your links and folders, organized

Save any web address, local folder, or network share as a "jump," group jumps into kits, and launch them with one click or a keyboard shortcut. The outcome: the resources you use daily are always exactly one action away.

Team sync — shared resource hubs

Share kits across your team so everyone works from the same set of links and shares — new hires get productive in minutes instead of asking where things live.

Time-saved tracking

JumpKit counts the clicks and minutes you didn't spend digging, so the value shows up in a number, not a feeling.

What Makes JumpKit Different

Local-first — your data stays on your machine

Cloud bookmark managers like Toby and Raindrop sync your entire workspace through their servers. JumpKit runs locally on your desktop; nothing about your links or file paths leaves your machine unless you choose team sync.

Files and folders, not just bookmarks

Browser bookmarks only handle URLs. JumpKit launches network shares, mapped drives, and local folders alongside web links — one tool for everything you open daily.

Built for teams, priced for everyone

Launchers like Raycast and Alfred are powerful but per-person power-user tools with paid tiers. JumpKit's core features — including the jumps organizer and launcher — are free, with paid plans adding team sync.

No bloat, nothing to learn

JumpKit installs in minutes and has no learning curve. It's a launcher that gets out of your way — not another workspace app demanding a workflow change.

Made for MSPs and IT teams

IT service providers use JumpKit to give every client a clean, organized set of resources — and partners can earn recurring commission on the seats they deploy.

How JumpKit Works

  1. Install the desktop app for Windows or macOS — takes about a minute.
  2. Add jumps: any URL, local folder, or network share, grouped into kits however your team thinks.
  3. Launch with one click or a hotkey — from the app or the system tray.
  4. Share (optional): publish a kit for your team so everyone has the same set.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my data leave my computer?

No. JumpKit is local-first — your jumps, folders, and usage history stay on your machine. Data only syncs if you explicitly enable team sharing.

What can a "jump" point to?

Any web URL, local folder, or network share. If you can open it on your machine, you can make it a jump.

Is JumpKit free?

The core features — jumps, kits, launcher, and time tracking — are free. Paid plans add team sync and sharing.

Which platforms are supported?

Windows and macOS. Both use the same kits and settings via team sync.
