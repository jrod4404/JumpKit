FAQ

Pricing questions, answered.

Is JumpKit really free?

Yes. JumpKit Free includes the core launcher: web links, local folders, categories, search, filters, hotkeys, and a personal ROI dashboard. It's free to download and use, with no credit card required.

What is the difference between Free and Unlimited?

JumpKit Free gives you 250 jump launches, 2 teams, 5 members, and 10 jumps per team. JumpKit Unlimited removes all limits — unlimited launches, unlimited teams and members, plus auto-archive, auto-backup, and team ROI dashboards.

Is pricing the same for Windows and Mac?

Yes. Pricing is per user regardless of platform. A JumpKit Unlimited license works on the machine you install it on, whether that's Windows or macOS.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. Billing is handled by Lemon Squeezy, and you can cancel anytime. Your jumps and data remain on your machine — JumpKit stores 100% locally, so there's no lock-in.

Is there a team or volume discount?

JumpKit is per user and affordable per seat, so most small teams simply buy Unlimited per person. For larger teams, contact us via Contact and we'll discuss volume pricing.
